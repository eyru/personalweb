研究兴趣Research Interests

  • 助听器场景的低延迟语音增强 —— 在 10 ms 延迟预算内平衡降噪量与语音可懂度。
  • Low-latency speech enhancement for hearing aids — balancing noise suppression and intelligibility within a 10 ms budget.
  • 主动降噪与通透模式的融合 —— 让降噪深度与环境感知连续可调，而非离散切换。
  • Fusion of active noise cancellation and transparency mode — continuous rather than discrete control between attenuation and awareness.
  • 数据驱动的个性化降噪 —— 从电测听图预测用户偏好的降噪策略。
  • Data-driven personalised noise reduction — predicting preferred strategies from audiograms.
